## Unreleased (draft, no version, no tag)

- Workflow states (workflows = swimlanes, decision of the user): every `workflows/*.yaml` (58 files) now carries in `document.metadata` the eight mandatory states (the first, `offen`, is the start state where tasks begin, confirmed by the user) `offen`, `wartet-auf-entscheidung`, `auf-anfrage`, `in-prüfung`, `pausiert`, `abgebrochen`, `erledigt`, `archiviert` (`freesynergy.net:states`) and the transition rules (`freesynergy.net:transitions`, the word `stationen` = the own steps of the flow; `pausiert` and `abgebrochen` from every live state, `archiviert` only from `erledigt` / `abgebrochen` and without way out); no step may carry a state name. New standard `workflows/ZUESTAENDE.md`; `scripts/check_workflows.py` is red for a missing or renamed state, a missing transition rule, a forbidden `archiviert` transition or a step named like a state; tests in `crates/fs-release/tests/workflows_zustaende.rs` and `workflows_zustaende_luecken.rs` (mutants: state deleted in every file, block removed in every file, state renamed, rule missing/incomplete, archiviert rules, step named like a state). No build logic changed (building belongs to the construction kit, not fs-tasks).
